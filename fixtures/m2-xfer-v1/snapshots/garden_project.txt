== Garden greenhouse project notes ==

The greenhouse monitoring project uses the spare raspberry pi. The sensors report soil moisture and temperature every five minutes.

Decided: the greenhouse watering pump runs on a soil moisture threshold, not a timer. Chosen threshold: pump activates below 30 percent moisture.

The greenhouse plants include tomatoes and basil. Monitors in the greenhouse are cheap e-ink displays, not studio monitors.

Budget note: the e-ink display cost 42 euros. The pump relay cost 18 euros.
