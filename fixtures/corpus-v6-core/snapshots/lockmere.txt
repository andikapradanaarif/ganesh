Lockmere canal lock operations log. Fictional operational log for evaluation use.

== Gate seals May 6 ==
The upstream gate seal replacement finished at 07:55 UTC with all bolts torqued.
Crew logged minor scoring on the lower hinge pin.

== Paddle gear May 7 ==
The ground paddle gear lubrication finished at 12:40 UTC.
The grease lines were flushed afterward.

== Water gauge May 9 ==
The downstream water gauge read 2.35 metres at 06:00 UTC before the first cycle.
The gauge board was cleaned and recalibrated by 08:15 UTC.

== Vessel count May 11 ==
Seventeen narrowboats passed through the lock during the day shift.
The final vessel cleared the lower gates at 18:20 UTC.

== Culvert May 13 ==
The culvert inspection began at 09:30 UTC and finished at 15:10 UTC.
Crew logged silt buildup along the northern channel wall.

== Balance beam May 15 ==
The cracked balance beam on gate two was swapped at 10:25 UTC.
The old beam was cut up and removed from the wharf.

== Ladder May 16 ==
The galvanised escape ladder installation began at 08:10 UTC.
The masonry anchors were still curing at end of shift.

== Bollards May 18 ==
Four new mooring bollards were cast and bedded at 13:30 UTC.
The concrete was still green; curing was not verified by end of shift.

== Pump house May 20 ==
The backpump impeller finished its vibration test at 11:05 UTC within tolerance.
The discharge valve seals were re-seated afterward.

== Bywash May 22 ==
The bywash weir drew a reading of 0.82 metres at 07:30 UTC.
Debris was cleared from the trash screen afterward.

== Signage May 25 ==
The lock keeper proposed repainting the depth signage at the north chamber wall.
The proposal was still awaiting approval at the end of the month.

== Gate seals May 27 ==
The downstream gate seal inspection finished at 09:45 UTC with no leaks found.
The lock reopened to traffic at 10:05 UTC.
