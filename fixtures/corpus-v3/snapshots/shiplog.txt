The Northwind is a fictional lighthouse supply vessel used as labeled synthetic material in the v3 evaluation packet. This is its voyage log.

== Voyage June 3 ==

The supply vessel Northwind departed Kirkwall at 06:00 UTC. The vessel arrived at the lighthouse platform at 09:15 UTC. The cargo inspection passed.

== Voyage June 4 ==

The supply vessel Northwind departed Kirkwall at 06:00 UTC. Heavy fog was reported in the channel. The vessel did not arrive at the platform before the weather window closed.

== Voyage June 5 ==

The supply vessel Northwind departed Kirkwall at 06:00 UTC. The cargo inspection was not carried out. The vessel returned by evening.
