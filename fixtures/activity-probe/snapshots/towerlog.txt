The Cedar Ridge fire lookout is a fictional forest service installation used as the frozen development fixture for negated-activity testing. This is its maintenance log.

== Visit August 3 ==

The inspection of tower T9 started at 10:00 UTC. The inspection finished at 11:20 UTC and the safety assessment passed.

== Visit August 4 ==

The inspection of tower T9 was scheduled. The inspection was cancelled before execution because of lightning risk.

== Visit August 5 ==

The inspection of tower T9 started at 10:00 UTC. The safety assessment was skipped. The crew left at noon.

== Visit August 6 ==

The inspection of tower T9 was planned for the morning. The inspection never took place. The access road was washed out.

== Visit August 7 ==

The inspection of tower T9 started at 10:00 UTC. No result was recorded for the safety assessment.

== Visit August 8 ==

The inspection of tower T9 finished at 11:00 UTC while the repair of the north stair continued.

== Visit August 9 ==

The repair of the north stair started at 09:00 UTC. The repair finished at 15:30 UTC.
